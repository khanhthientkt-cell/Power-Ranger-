How to play 🦊🔬

  1. 🎴 Tap the card to start a tiny experiment.
  2. 🔮 Make a guess — that's what scientists do first!
  3. 🔍 Count, compare, or find the pattern to discover the answer.
  4. ⭐ Discover it to win a star and fill the bar.
  5. 🎟️ Level up to collect a cute sticker!

For grown-ups: Every card secretly teaches a quantitative-research idea made tiny — making a guess (hypothesis), counting (data), reading a chart (visualization), most-common (mode), fair-sharing (average), patterns (trends), and chance (probability). Foxy explains each one in kid words. The goal: turn big ideas into little "aha!" moments.
