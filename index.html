How to play 🦊

  1. 🎴 Tap the card to draw a puzzle.
  2. 🧮 Count the pictures or pick more / fewer.
  3. 👆 Tap the big answer button you think is right.
  4. ⭐ Get it right to win a star and fill the bar.
  5. 🎉 Fill the bar to LEVEL UP — it gets a little trickier!

For grown-ups: Sit with your child the first few rounds. The fox reads each question aloud, so pre-readers can play on their own too. Tap 🔁 anytime to hear it again.
